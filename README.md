## Hi, I’m [Manav Mishra](https://www.linkedin.com/in/manavmishra/), creator of [Zero Slop](https://zero-slop.ai/).

I’m a product builder and technologist who has worked across engineering, product, design, and business leadership at Intel, Microsoft, Google, HPE, Silver Peak, and MSCI.

At Microsoft, I helped build large-scale anti-spam, anti-phishing, anti-malware, anti-spyware, and reputation systems for Office, Windows, and browsers, including technology that became part of [Microsoft SmartScreen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_SmartScreen).

I’ve also helped build and launch products and platforms including [Windows 7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windows_7), [Windows Live Mesh (OneDrive)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windows_Live_Mesh), [Google Analytics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Analytics), [HPE Helion Stackato](https://www.hpe.com/psnow/doc/a00001265enw), [Silver Peak EdgeConnect SD-WAN](https://arubanetworking.hpe.com/techdocs/sdwan/), and the platform powering [AI-driven risk and portfolio intelligence for institutional investors at MSCI](https://www.msci.com/data-and-analytics/risk-management-solutions/ai-portfolio-insights).

Today, I’m building an agentic AI startup in stealth mode and open-sourcing tools I find useful along the way.

### Current projects

- [**Zero Slop**](https://zero-slop.ai/) — open-source editing that finds canned AI writing and rewrites it in your voice.
- [**GitHub**](https://github.com/manavmishra) — experiments, agent skills, and open-source AI tools.

I’m especially interested in agentic systems, AI infrastructure, context and harness design, enterprise AI, and the tools that make AI more useful in real-world workflows.
